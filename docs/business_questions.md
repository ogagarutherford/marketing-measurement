# Business Questions — Velora Marketing Measurement

Velora is a fictional direct-to-consumer skincare brand selling online across Germany's 16 states
(synthetic data, January–June 2025). It spends about €240k a month on Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, DV360 and affiliates,
plus email to its own customers.

Leadership asks one question: **where should the next euro of marketing spend go?** That breaks into:

1. **Performance:** what do we spend, what do we get, and how efficient is each channel (CTR, CPC, CPM, CVR, CAC, ROAS, MER)?
2. **Measurement gaps:** why do ad platforms, GA4 and our order system all report different numbers, and which should we trust for what?
3. **Attribution:** how does the credit each channel receives change under last-click, first-click, linear and position-based models, and what does that tell us about each channel's role?
4. **Incrementality:** Meta reports a ROAS above 3. How many orders does Meta actually *cause*? (Answered with a pre-registered geo test.)
5. **Economics:** after product, shipping and payment costs, which customers and channels pay back, and how fast (contribution margin, LTV, payback)?
6. **Privacy:** what do consent, GDPR, the Digital Markets Act and Apple's SKAdNetwork mean for what we can measure?

The final deliverable is a budget recommendation backed by incrementality evidence, not platform-reported ROAS alone.
